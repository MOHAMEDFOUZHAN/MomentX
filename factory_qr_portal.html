Recommended Factory Placement & Printing Guidelines

Standee Size

For entrance roll-up standees (2.5 ft × 6 ft), make the QR code at least 25 cm × 25 cm so visitors can scan from 3–5 meters away.

Acrylic Table Stands

For reception and tasting counters, print on A5/A4 photo paper or back-lit acrylic boards with minimum 10 cm × 10 cm QR size.

Lighting & Finish

Use a matte or semi-matte lamination on vinyl to prevent glare from factory ceiling lights from interfering with phone cameras.

Error Correction Level H

All generated QR codes use Level H (30% ECC), guaranteeing reliable scanning even with dust, slight scratches, or reflections.
